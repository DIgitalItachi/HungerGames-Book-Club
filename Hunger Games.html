    Would You Rather: Arena Edition

    Everyone picks · explain your choice · group votes the best argument for a point
    OR

    Sponsor Gifts

    One round · 2 points to the gift the group likes best

    You're a sponsor and you can send one silver parachute to a tribute. Choose the tribute and the item, then make your case in 30 seconds. Could you have changed how the book ended?

    • Burn medicine to Katniss before the fire wall
    • Sleep syrup or a weapon to Peeta
    • A full meal to Rue and Katniss's camp
    • Something to Cato, Foxface, or Thresh to flip the story

    Which District Are You?

    For laughs · no points

    Everyone assigns everyone else a district based on job, hobbies, or personality, and defends it. District 3 for the tech person, 10 for whoever grills, 4 for anyone who fishes, 1 for whoever brought the fancy snacks. Majority wins.
